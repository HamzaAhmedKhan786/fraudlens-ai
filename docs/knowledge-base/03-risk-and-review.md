# How are risk levels defined?
Low is 0–29, Medium 30–59, High 60–79, and Critical 80–100. Critical requires immediate human review according to organizational policy.

# Does Critical mean confirmed fraud?
No. It means the configured indicators warrant urgent review, not that fraud is proven.

# Can AI approve a case?
No. Versioned deterministic rules calculate risk. LLMs may describe cited findings, but authorized human reviewers own consequential decisions.
