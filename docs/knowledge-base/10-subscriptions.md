# What does my subscription include?
Open Subscription & billing to see the workspace's configured plan, subscription status, usage limits, usage consumed, and remaining allowances. Limits include investigations, email processing, OCR, storage, and GPU inference. Your workspace administrator controls the selected plan.

# Are payments enabled?
Payment availability depends on the deployment configuration. Stripe integration supports test mode; a displayed unavailable state means payment setup is incomplete. Do not assume that viewing a plan activates a paid subscription.
